Who we are

History Unsealed is a Facebook Page that publishes posts and short videos about historical mysteries. The app "History Unsealed Publisher" is an internal tool used only to publish content to our own Facebook Page.

What data we collect

The app does not collect, store or sell personal data from visitors or users. It only uses the Facebook Graph API to publish posts to our own Page, add comments on our own posts, and read aggregate engagement counts (reactions, comments, shares) on our own posts.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the app use no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data, there is nothing to delete. If you have any question or request, email historyvaultmedia8@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
